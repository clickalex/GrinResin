# GrinRex Resin — Studio & Business Source Reference

*The complete studio foundation behind the presentation. Planning ranges come from the source document and must be verified with supplier quotations and local compliance review.*

## 1. Market & model

- India-based home studio; personalized resin gifts and keepsakes.
- Channels: Instagram, WhatsApp Business, local exhibitions, gift-store consignment, online marketplaces, and an owned website.
- Positioning: affordable-to-premium customized handmade resin.
- Differentiation: personalization depth and finish discipline — not catalogue size or lowest price.

## 2. Revenue streams (in sequence)

1. Direct product sales (launch)
2. Personalization surcharge (launch)
3. Gift combinations & bundles
4. Wedding & occasion orders
5. Corporate gifting
6. DIY kits
7. Workshops
8. Selected wholesale

Streams 3–8 open only after the core process holds: stable unit costs, dependable cure windows, and clean dispatch records.

## 4. Core bench & tools

Measuring scale; mixing cups and stirring sticks; silicone molds (multipurpose first); heat gun or mini torch; hand drill; sandpaper (progressive grits); pliers; beakers; protective storage; nitrile gloves; respirator mask; ventilation.

## 5. Core material palette

Epoxy resin and hardener; mica powders; pigment pastes; alcohol inks; gold foil; dried botanicals; fine glitter; resin dyes; glass beads; keychain rings, earring hooks, bails, magnets; protective packaging (boxes, wrap, tissue, ribbon).

## 6. Pricing framework

Formula: **material + labor + packaging + overhead + fees + profit**.

Cost components per SKU: resin & hardener; pigments & inclusions; hardware & packaging; tool amortization; labor & electricity; wastage, payment fees & shipping.

Category material bands (source): small ₹15–₹40 · medium ₹30–₹100 · large ₹60–₹250 · premium ₹150–₹1,000+.

## 7. Production process (16 steps)

Design concept → mold selection → workspace preparation → measure resin & hardener → slow mixing → pigments/inclusions → pour → bubble removal → cure → demold → trim & sand → drill/assembly → polish & hardware → quality inspection → protective packaging → storage & dispatch.

## 8. Quality-control checks

Full cure · surface smoothness · bubble level · cracks · dimensions · personalization accuracy · hardware security · edge safety · packaging protection.

## 9. Safety boundaries

- Follow resin manufacturer instructions and SDS guidance; read labels before first use.
- Wear nitrile gloves and a suitable mask; work with cross-ventilation; never mix indoors near children or pets.
- Sand and trim cured pieces with dust control (damp sanding or mask + extraction).
- No food-contact or heat-use claims unless the full material-and-design system is verified for that use.
- Store pigments, inks, and mixed resin separately from living spaces; dispose per local rules.
