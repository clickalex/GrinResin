# GrinRex Resin — Investor Brief

*A measured case for growth. This brief deliberately makes no unverified market-size, revenue, valuation, or return claims.*

## The one-page logic

GrinRex Resin develops from a disciplined direct-to-consumer keepsake collection into a broader gifting and lifestyle platform — with future capability in wedding and corporate gifting, selected wholesale, workshops, DIY kits, and specialist collections. Growth is funded against evidence, not enthusiasm.

## Capital reference (source plan)

The source plan defines a controlled setup of **₹10,000–₹14,000** for essential equipment, safety gear, multipurpose molds, core materials, protective packaging, and early presentation.

> This is a starting cost reference — **not** a valuation, revenue forecast, funding ask, or return claim.

## Staged use of funds

| Stage | Purpose | Trigger to unlock |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Phase 1 | Safety, ventilation, scale, molds, restricted palette | Workspace exists and test pours pass |
| Phase 2 | Material stock, launch photography, gift packaging for the 12-product edit | Unit costs and cure windows are stable |
| Phase 3 | Capacity tools, packaging upgrades, small content budget | Order flow is regular and contribution is positive per SKU |

## Milestone logic

1. **Prove demand** on a narrowed launch edit — orders arrive without paid push.
2. **Prove margin** — every SKU passes the pricing guardrail with wastage included.
3. **Prove repeatability** — quality passes on batch work, not single hero pours.
4. **Prove capacity** — dispatch promises hold when volume doubles.
5. Only then expand catalogue, channels, and B2B commitments.

## Expansion lanes (source-defined)

Jewelry brand · home décor · wedding collection · corporate gifting · pet memorials · gaming collection · DIY kits · workshops · wholesale · custom molds · supplies store · design marketplace.

## What serious future materials must add

- Verified sales history and product-level margin tables.
- Operating capacity data (cure calendar, dispatch throughput).
- Customer cohorts, repeat and referral rates.
- Working-capital needs and specific use-of-funds sizing.
- Compliance notes for packaging, claims, and any food/heat-adjacent product lines.

## Why this restraint is the pitch

A studio that refuses to inflate numbers in its own presentation is applying the same discipline to its costs, its cure times, and its promises to customers. GrinRex Resin is built to be auditable from the first pour.
