# GrinRex Resin — Business Overview

*A source-backed presentation reference. All figures are planning inputs, not quotations or commitments.*

## The brand in one line

**GrinRex Resin turns personal moments into tactile keepsakes, for gift-givers and memory-makers who value meaning over sameness.**

## What the business is

GrinRex Resin is a personalized handmade gifting and keepsake brand for the Indian market, transforming names, photographs, flowers, dates, and occasions into durable resin pieces. The model is a home-based studio with custom orders, online sales, and local selling — deliberately compact at the start so that safety, repeatability, and finish quality come before equipment breadth or catalogue size.

## Why now

- Personalized gifting is growing as buyers seek meaning over sameness in festivals, weddings, birthdays, and memorials.
- Small-batch resin work carries high personalization depth at low starting capital.
- Social commerce (Instagram, WhatsApp Business) lets a disciplined maker reach direct customers without retail overhead.

## The opening range

The source plan calls for a controlled 10–20 product launch through Instagram, WhatsApp Business, local exhibitions, gift shops, and online marketplaces. Everyday gifts, personal keepsakes, and functional gifting pieces create a coherent starting range:

1. **Everyday gifts** — keychains, bookmarks, fridge magnets, earrings.
2. **Personal keepsakes** — nameplates, photo blocks, jewelry dishes, ring dishes.
3. **Home & gifting décor** — coasters, phone stands, compact trays, curated sets.

## Sequencing rule

Individual sales and custom orders come first. Gift combos, wedding orders, corporate gifting, wholesale, workshops, and DIY kits follow **only once the studio system is dependable**. Customization — not catalogue size or lowest-price positioning — is the core differentiation.

## Standing constraints

- No food-contact or heat-use claims unless the material and complete product design are verified for that use.
- Every SKU is tested for safety, unit cost, production time, quality, demand, packaging, and dispatch risk before it joins the sales range.
- All planning figures (setup ranges, category material bands) require supplier quotations before commercial use.
