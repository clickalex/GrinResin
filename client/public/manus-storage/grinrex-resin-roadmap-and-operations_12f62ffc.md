# GrinRex Resin — Roadmap & Internal Operations

*How the studio moves from first pour to a durable system — and what it tracks on the way.*

## 1. Setup economics (source plan)

- **Initial setup estimate: ₹10,000–₹14,000.**
  - Essential tools: ₹6,000–₹8,000
  - Initial raw materials: ₹4,000–₹6,000
- These are planning ranges, **not supplier quotations**. Pressure pots, rotary tools, and polishing machines remain later-stage decisions tied to demand evidence.

## 2. Roadmap

| Phase | Title | Exit evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | Starter setup | Safe bench, ventilation, PPE routine; first test pours pass |
| 02 | MVP catalogue | 10–12 launch items photographed, costed, and selling direct |
| 03 | Customization system | Proof-approval flow for names, photos, flowers, wedding details |
| 04 | Controlled expansion | Toward 50 demand-validated products, bundles, seasonal drops |
| 05 | Business development | Selected wholesale, corporate gifting, repeat programs, marketplace tests |
| 06 | Scale with proof | SOPs, bulk buying, dedicated workstations, stronger QC, B2B capacity |

Each phase gates on evidence — no phase opens because a catalogue sounds big.

## 3. Order workflow

1. **Enquiry** — Instagram/WhatsApp/marketplace message logged in the order sheet within the hour.
2. **Proof** — name spelling, photo crop, and placement approved before any resin moves.
3. **Bench queue** — piece enters production with its SKU card (materials, mold, labor minutes).
4. **Release** — QC passed, boxed, photographed, dispatched; the card records cost, outcome, and delivery date.

## 4. The production route (16 steps)

Design concept · mold selection · workspace preparation · measure resin & hardener · slow mixing · pigments & inclusions · pour · bubble removal · cure · demold · trim & sand · drill/assembly · polish & hardware · quality inspection · protective packaging · storage & dispatch.

Customer-facing status compresses these into eight states: **Order received → Design proof → Mold, mix & pour → Curing → Demold & finish → Quality inspection → Protected packing → Dispatched.**

## 5. Quality control

Every piece: full cure, surface smoothness, bubbles, cracks, dimensions, personalization accuracy, hardware security, edge safety, packaging protection. Failed pieces re-enter as learning — recorded as wastage against the SKU.

## 6. Safety operations

- Follow manufacturer instructions and SDS; gloves and suitable mask for mixing and sanding; cross-ventilation.
- Controlled workspace access away from children and pets.
- No food-contact or heat-use claims unless the material and full product design are verified.
- Material batch log: resin lot numbers and cure conditions kept per production week.

## 7. Metrics managed with every SKU

Material cost per product · labor time and cost · packaging, fees & damage rate · wastage percentage · gross & net contribution · order volume & average order value · repeat or referral demand · customer acquisition cost.

**Selling-price guardrail:** material + labor + packaging + overhead + fees + profit — recompute monthly and after every supplier price change.

## 8. Pricing discipline

- Small pieces: entry band, high volume, fast cure.
- Keepsakes and photo work: mid band, proofing overhead included in labor.
- Large casts and premium preserves: deep-pour rules, longer cure, priced for patience.
- Corporate/wedding lots: minimum order quantities, deposit before pour, and a capacity check against the cure calendar before quoting a date.
