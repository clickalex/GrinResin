# Chosen Approach: Lacquered Portfolio

## Design Movement

**Contemporary editorial luxury with the cinematic depth of polished lacquer and product-portfolio storytelling.** The design borrows the restraint of a high-end print lookbook and the atmosphere of a craft-film title sequence, without becoming sterile or overly corporate.

## Core Principles

1. **Story before detail:** The web experience progresses from emotional promise to commercial logic, then operating proof and investor readiness.
2. **Resin as a visual material:** Transparency, reflections, pooled color, and suspended botanical detail guide surfaces, images, and dividers.
3. **Asymmetric editorial pacing:** Tall chapter numbers, offset content blocks, and a fixed progress rail replace generic centered landing-page sections.
4. **Selective evidence:** High-impact metrics and operational facts appear in restrained data cards, leaving generous breathing room around the core narrative.

## Color Philosophy

The foundation is **ink-black**, evoking the depth of cured resin and allowing photography to feel gallery-lit. **Honey amber** is the ownable highlight—warm enough to suggest gifting and polished enough for investor communication. A muted **sage mineral** and **chalk white** are secondary tones that soften the darkness and echo dried flowers, paper labels, and hand-finished objects. Glow is restrained and physical-looking, as if light is catching an object rather than a digital effect.

## Layout Paradigm

The site is a **vertical chapter reel**. A slim fixed left rail on desktop holds the brand mark, chapter numbers, and presentation progress. The main column uses deliberately offset text/image compositions: editorial labels occupy one edge, content occupies another, and wide visual panels interrupt the narrative at important transitions. On mobile, the rail compresses into a compact top bar and chapter chips.

## Signature Elements

1. **Amber memory thread:** A fine, irregular vertical amber line travels through the presentation and marks the current chapter.
2. **Cast specimen cards:** Semi-transparent, layered panels with subtle inner borders that look like clear resin casts holding labels, numbers, and details.
3. **Amber chapter numerals:** Oversized two-digit numbers punctuate the sequence and make the presentation feel authored rather than templated.

## Interaction Philosophy

Navigation feels like moving through a premium printed portfolio. The chapter rail scrolls smoothly to sections; document cards expand into readable drawers; and buttons provide clear destination language rather than generic CTAs. There are no novelty interactions—the brand earns trust through clarity and calm precision.

## Animation

Sections reveal with short opacity and upward-transform transitions, staggered by 50–70ms. The memory thread subtly brightens as a chapter enters view. Cards lift only 2–4px on hover, with a soft amber rim light. All nonessential motion is disabled under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Typography System

**Cormorant Garamond** provides the expressive editorial display voice for feature headlines, especially italic fragments. **DM Sans** provides operational readability for labels, metrics, documents, and body copy. Headlines use a high contrast between large serif words and compact uppercase metadata; body text stays measured and open.

## Brand Essence

**GrinRex Resin turns personal moments into tactile keepsakes, for gift-givers and memory-makers who value meaning over sameness.**

**Personality:** Considered, warm, precise.

## Brand Voice

Headlines are concise, sensory, and emotionally specific. CTAs direct visitors to the next proof point, while microcopy explains the process without hype.

Examples:

> “A name, a flower, a moment—cast to keep.”

> “See how a small maker studio becomes a repeatable gifting business.”

## Wordmark & Logo

The mark is an abstract **G** formed from a resin droplet and a single botanical petal, constructed as an amber, rounded geometric symbol. It is paired with an elegant wordmark that uses a custom-feeling serif treatment rather than a default sans-serif setting.

## Signature Brand Color

**Cast Honey — #D9983A.** This amber-orange is warm, ownable, and readable on the ink-black presentation background.

## Presentation Sequence

1. **Cover / Brand promise** — What GrinRex Resin is and why it exists.
2. **Opportunity** — Why personalized gifting, keepsakes, and small-batch resin objects offer a focused opening.
3. **Business system** — Revenue streams and selected MVP product families.
4. **Product strategy** — From a 150-product idea library to a disciplined 12-product launch collection.
5. **Operating model** — Materials, production flow, quality control, and safety boundaries.
6. **Roadmap** — The staged path from setup through controlled scale.
7. **Internal playbook** — Pricing guardrails, metrics, order workflow, and risk controls.
8. **Investor brief** — Use of funds, traction milestones, investment logic, and what the business is building toward.
9. **Document library / close** — Readable in-site documents and downloadable references in their intended order.

## Style Decisions

- The main content must remain sequential, with document access appearing only after the relevant chapter has introduced its context.
- The investor section must make no unverified market-size, revenue, valuation, or return claims.
- The site uses **ink, amber, mineral sage, and chalk**; no purple gradients, neon colors, or generic blue SaaS styling.
- Rounded corners stay subtle and functional; long panels use straight or lightly curved edges to retain editorial discipline.
- Imagery must visibly connect to resin keepsakes, studio tools, botanical suspension, names or photographs, gift packaging, or finished handmade objects; generic lifestyle images are excluded.
- The amber memory thread and chapter numerals function as a signature navigation motif on every major section, not merely as subtle decoration.
- Cast specimen cards use translucent layering, fine inner borders, amber indexing, and restrained museum-label typography to resemble labeled objects preserved in clear resin.
- The amber droplet-and-petal **G** mark and elegant serif wordmark act as a high-visibility brand anchor in the hero, chapter rail, and final close; they are not treated as small utility branding.
- **Cast Honey — #D9983A** remains reserved for chapter numerals, italic headline emphasis, primary actions, and fine memory-thread or index details.
